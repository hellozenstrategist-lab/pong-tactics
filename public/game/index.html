Movement & fighting

WASD moves. Mouse aims. Left-click strikes. Q parries. E wards. Space / Shift dashes. F guards. R counters after a perfect parry.

Typing under pressure

Enter focuses Jev; Enter submits. Escape returns to WASD. While typing, arrow keys move and right-click steers. Alt Q/E/F parry/ward/guard without clearing your text. Left-click on the arena still strikes.

Channel defense

Taking a hit during thinking, casting, or scanning rolls d6. 4–6 saves the channel. Guard improves that to 2–6. A ward absorbs one hit. A failed roll deals damage and cancels the channel.

Clashes & objectives

Colliding attacks trigger a tactic duel: Push > Break > Redirect > Push. Choose 1/2/3 or type the tactic. In Flag Hunt, reach the relay, scan four times, then physically hold the verified flag site for 4s.

Heavy profiles have longer game windups. Actual API latency adds vulnerable thinking time. Your human can still move and strike while the Familiar reasons.

Tab opens the clue panel without pausing. swap summons the offline reserve through an interruptible 5s channel. In Flag Hunt, death respawns you; kills alone do not win. These are solo local matches, not authoritative multiplayer.

The objective is a synthetic deduction puzzle. Real CTF service exploitation and production isolation are not implemented.
