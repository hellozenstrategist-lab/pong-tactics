Your Familiar

Spells aren't instant: your Familiar thinks, then channels. Type freely — “open a void rift”, “heal me”, “create a false echo” — or press 1, 2 or 3. Swift, Balanced and Deep Familiars trade cast time for power. Type swap to summon the offline reserve (5 s channel).

Channel defense

If you're hit while your Familiar is thinking, casting or scanning, a d6 decides whether the channel survives: 4–6 normally, 2–6 while guarding. A ward blocks one hit outright.

Clashes

When strikes or spells collide, both fighters lock. Pick before the bar runs out: Push beats Break, Break beats Redirect, Redirect beats Push. No pick loses.

Flag Hunt

Reach the Noetic Relay and scan four times to decode the flag site, then stand on it for 4 uncontested seconds. Kills don't win; you respawn after 4 s.

Offline prototype. The offline Familiar is a keyword interpreter, not an LLM, and opponents use local policies. Matches are solo and run in your browser. Flag Hunt is a symbolic deduction puzzle with no real exploitation or live services. Cast times are game balance, not claims about any model's speed; with an API Familiar, real response time adds to your vulnerable thinking window.
